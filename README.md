### Andrea Tonelli

Physician (MBChB) and final-year PhD candidate in cardiovascular biomaterials at the University of Cape Town.
I build the computational tools my research needs, and production software by directing AI coding agents.

**Research software**

- [growth-tunnel-analysis](https://github.com/andtoni/growth-tunnel-analysis): pore-network extraction and
  analysis for micro-CT scans of porous biomaterials (Python, PoreSpy, OpenPNM). MIT licensed, archived on
  Zenodo ([doi:10.5281/zenodo.20308262](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.20308262)); the method behind
  *Materials Today Bio* 2026;39:103377.
- [microct-superresolution](https://github.com/andtoni/microct-superresolution): deep-learning
  super-resolution and segmentation for micro-CT of porous scaffolds (PyTorch, nnU-Net, Snakemake).
  Research code for a manuscript in preparation.

**Software engineering**

- [finance-sync-core](https://github.com/andtoni/finance-sync-core): a headless service that records bank and
  brokerage data as immutable facts in PostgreSQL, reconciles card authorisations with settlements, and
  delivers changes with a receipt for every write (TypeScript, about 500 tests).
- [wealthfolio-fork](https://github.com/andtoni/wealthfolio-fork): my fork of the open-source portfolio tracker,
  extended with bank-fed spending and configurable budget cycles (Rust, TypeScript).
- [agentic-engineering](https://github.com/andtoni/agentic-engineering): how I build with AI coding agents,
  with the numbers and the controls.
